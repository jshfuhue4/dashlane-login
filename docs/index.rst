Dashlane is a leading password manager that helps users securely store passwords, payment information, and personal data. Understanding the Dashlane login process is essential for maintaining secure access to your digital accounts.

What Is Dashlane?
-----------------

Dashlane is a password management solution that offers:

* Secure password storage
* Password generation
* Autofill capabilities
* Dark Web Monitoring
* Secure password sharing
* Multi-device synchronization

How to Log In to Dashlane
-------------------------

To access your Dashlane account:

1. Open the official Dashlane login page.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Verify your identity using your Master Password or biometric authentication.
4. Complete two-factor authentication (2FA) if enabled.
5. Access your encrypted password vault.

Common Login Issues
-------------------

Forgot Master Password
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

If you forget your Master Password, use Account Recovery if it has been enabled. Otherwise, you may need to reset your account.

Two-Factor Authentication Problems
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Possible solutions include:

* Checking your authenticator app
* Using backup recovery codes
* Ensuring your device has internet access

Browser Extension Issues
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

If the Dashlane extension is not working:

* Update your browser.
* Refresh the page.
* Clear browser cache.
* Reinstall the extension.
